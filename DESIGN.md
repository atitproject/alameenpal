## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, dependable, and precision-driven visual identity tailored for commercial trade, freight distribution, and supply-chain logistics. Grounded in the corporate heritage of the brand identity, the system balances two complementary forces: deep, grounded burgundy-red conveying stability, heritage, and uncompromising commercial integrity, paired alongside an active, vital leaf-green symbolizing agile movement, freshness of consumer goods, and seamless distribution across regional networks.

The overarching design aesthetic fuses **Corporate Modernism** with crisp **Tactile Precision**. Rather than generic flat dashboards or overly sterile enterprise portals, the experience projects structural solidity, high-contrast legibility, and generous breathing room. Visual interfaces emphasize clear inventory metrics, fleet tracking indicators, transparent order pipelines, and trade catalogue presentations. The experience instills absolute trust for B2B partners, commercial distributors, retail merchants, and operations supervisors navigating large volumes of logistics data across desktop and handheld warehouse devices.

## Colors

The color palette is meticulously calibrated from the brand's signature mark, harmonizing high-contrast crimson with balanced botanical and olive tones against architectural neutrals:

- **Primary (`#A81D22` - Heritage Crimson):** The anchor for brand authority, hero call-to-actions, core status highlights, and foundational header accents. Extended into deep burgundy tints (`#7B1115`) for pressed interactive states and soft rose whites (`#FDF2F2`) for critical alert backdrops.
- **Secondary (`#5D9E14` - Active Dispatch Green):** Represents kinetic momentum, confirmed logistics checkpoints, fulfillment statuses, and inventory health. Used for active badges, delivery track highlights, and secondary operational triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#0F5132` - Deep Evergreen):** Provides stable contrast for formal corporate seals, verified partner credentials, and financial metrics within ledger views.
- **Neutral (`#1E242B` - Graphite Slate):** A charcoal-slate base that eliminates harsh pure-black eye strain. Complemented by layered neutral tiers:
  - Surface base: `#F8FAFC` (pure porcelain background)
  - Card & Container surface: `#FFFFFF` (crisp white)
  - Surface subtle: `#F1F5F9` (cool dove gray for data grid alternations and side panels)
  - Border subtle: `#E2E8F0` (clean, razor-thin structural dividers)
  - Text secondary: `#64748B` (refined informational copy)

## Typography

The typographic architecture is engineered for bilingual Arabic and Latin operational fluency. It pairs rhythmic proportion with high data-density legibility:

- **Primary Latin Display & Data Engine:** Plus Jakarta Sans delivers geometric precision with warm, contemporary humanism, retaining supreme clarity across dense inventory tables, SKU labels, and numeric consignment dashboards.
- **Bilingual Arabic Pairing Directive:** In multilingual applications, map Latin levels directly to **Cairo** or **Tajawal** at matched weights (`700 Bold` for headlines, `500 Medium` for interactive items, and `400 Regular` for running inventory descriptions). Line-height in Arabic contexts expands by `+4px` across body levels to accommodate calligraphic ascenders and descenders seamlessly.
- **RTL Rhythm & Alignment:** All numerical codes, shipment barcodes, and timestamps remain tabular monospaced or structured LTR when paired with Arabic metadata, ensuring immediate visual scanning for logistics staff.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system utilizes an 8-point harmonic layout grid adapted for data-heavy commercial applications and responsive client-facing catalogues:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Allows comfortable asymmetric distributions (e.g., 3-column persistent logistics filter navigation alongside a 9-column dynamic consignment overview).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `gutter: 1rem` and `margin: 1.5rem`. Secondary panels collapse into dismissible slide-out side sheets.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `gutter-mobile: 0.75rem` and `margin-mobile: 1rem`. Tables reflow into dedicated stackable transaction cards with swipeable action triggers.
- **Internal Density Controls:** Spacing inside operational components favors compact efficiency (`space-xs` and `space-sm`), while editorial marketing displays and customer billing summaries expand utilizing `space-lg` and `space-xl` for an elevated, orderly enterprise aesthetic.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through crisp structural stratification combining subtle low-contrast boundaries with warm ambient drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Background canvas (`#F8FAFC`) with no shadow. Structural separation relies on precise 1px hairline dividers (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** Solid white containers (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and an ambient tinted shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(30, 36, 43, 0.05), 0 1px 2px rgba(30, 36, 43, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating & Hover States):** Applied to active order items, dropdown menus, and hover states of commercial catalogues: `0 8px 16px -4px rgba(30, 36, 43, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(30, 36, 43, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Dispatch Sheets & Floating Trackers):** Deep elevation for critical modals, manifest popups, and delivery confirmation dialogs: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(30, 36, 43, 0.12), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(30, 36, 43, 0.04)` complemented by a semi-transparent dark graphite scrim (`rgba(30, 36, 43, 0.5)`).
- **Brand Accent Elevation:** High-priority confirmation alerts or live tracking pins incorporate a specialized secondary glow: `0 4px 14px rgba(93, 158, 20, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The shape system employs balanced, modern rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2`) that reflects physical packaging contours, cargo containers, and modern logistics hardware without appearing overly casual:

- **Micro Components (Badges, Chips, Small Buttons):** `0.375rem` (6px) to `0.5rem` (8px) radius, preserving sharp legibility in compact footprints.
- **Standard Controls (Inputs, Primary Buttons, Select Triggers):** `0.5rem` (8px) radius, delivering a sturdy, tactile interaction surface.
- **Surfaces & Cards:** `1rem` (16px) radius (`rounded-lg`), framing inventory groups and shipping summaries with a clean, cohesive boundary.
- **Modals & Hero Banners:** `1.5rem` (24px) radius (`rounded-xl`), creating distinct, executive focal points.
- **Status Pills & Operational Badges:** Full pill-radius (`9999px`) reserved specifically for live shipment status indicators (e.g., "In Transit", "Dispatched", "Received").

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Brand Crimson):** Solid `#A81D22` background, white text, 8px corner radius, bold font. Hover transitions smoothly to `#8F161B` with subtle elevation. Focus states introduce a 3px ring of `#A81D22` at 30% opacity.
- **Secondary (Active Logistics Green):** Solid `#5D9E14` background, white text. Designated for positive completions, order submissions, and driver dispatch triggers. Hover deepens to `#4E8410`.
- **Outline / Secondary Neutral:** Crisp white background with a 1.5px border in `#CBD5E1`, text in `#1E242B`. Hover state applies `#F1F5F9` background with border color darkening to `#94A3B8`.
- **Ghost Buttons:** Transparent background with `#A81D22` or `#1E242B` text; reserved for row-level actions within data tables.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Pill-shaped (`9999px`), 4px vertical by 10px horizontal padding, label-sm weight.
  - *Delivered / In-Stock:* `#ECFDF5` background with `#0F5132` text and border `#A7F3D0`.
  - *Active Dispatch / In-Transit:* `#F7FEE7` background with `#4D7C0F` text and border `#D9F99D`.
  - *Processing / Pending:* `#FFFBEB` background with `#B45309` text and border `#FDE68A`.
  - *Delayed / Discrepancy:* `#FEF2F2` background with `#991B1B` text and border `#FECACA`.

### Cards & Data Containers
- **Product & Consignment Cards:** Pure white ground, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, 16px corner radius, internal padding of `space-md` (16px) to `space-lg` (24px). Header area features a distinct visual separation with bold item nomenclature, SKU tag, and price/unit indicator.
- **Metric KPI Widgets:** Minimalist cards showcasing top-line logistics indicators (e.g., "Active Shipments", "Total Tonnage") with an accent left border (or right border in RTL) colored in Primary Crimson or Secondary Green.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Fields:** Height of 44px (touch-friendly for logistics mobile devices), `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px border `#CBD5E1`, 8px corner radius. Placeholder text in `#94A3B8`. Active focus shifts border to `#A81D22` with a 3px matching glow (`rgba(168, 29, 34, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18px bounding box with 4px radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios. Active checkmark features solid `#A81D22` fill with white indicator glyph.

### Lists & Manifest Data Tables
- Clean tabular data lists with subtle zebra striping using `#F8FAFC` on alternating rows. 
- Row hover triggers an immediate `#F1F5F9` highlight with a subtle indicator line on the leading edge.
- Compact cell paddings (12px vertical, 16px horizontal) provide maximal vertical overview for large consignment manifests.
