ABOUT US

Rooted in Palestine. Open to the world.

Based in Ramallah, Al Amin Pal is a food trading company since 2006 with a simple belief: lasting partnerships begin with understanding people.

We bring this perspective to the brands we select, the relationships we build, and our work in the Palestinian market.

Our commitment begins with care in what we choose and how we work—with our suppliers, local businesses, and the communities we are part of.
